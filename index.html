Questions

What do I need to rent a car?

You must be 23 or older, have held a full licence for at least 2 years, and pay with a credit card in your own name. Foreign licences not in English need an International Driving Permit or certified translation.

How are daily, weekly and monthly rates worked out?

We price each part of your rental at the best rate. 7 days costs the same as 6, and 30 days costs about 22 days. You always see the total before you pay.

What is the deposit for?

We hold S$500 on your card when you collect the car. It is released after inspection, once any final charges are settled.

What if I return the car with a low battery?

Below 20% we charge a S$50 recharging fee. Add the Pre-paid charging extra and you can return it at any level.

What happens if there is damage?

Staff record damage with photos at inspection. What you pay is capped by your protection: S$3,000 with Basic, S$1,000 with Standard, and nothing with Full. You can appeal any charge from your bills page.
